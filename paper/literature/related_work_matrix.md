# Related Work Matrix and Positioning

| Line of work | Representative papers | What they solve | What they miss | How we relate | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Closed-set sonar CNNs | Valdenegro-Toro (2016, 2017), Williams (2016), Singh and Valdenegro-Toro (2021), Valdenegro-Toro et al. (2025) | High accuracy on cataloged FLS and SAS target classes under speckle and pose shifts | Forced closed-set softmax predictions when novel debris appears | Uses MD-FLS Watertank (`ARIS Explorer 3000`) and ResNet-50 under an open-set near-OOD protocol | background / dataset |
| Probabilistic and OOD calibration | Scheirer et al. (2013), Bendale and Boult (2016), Hendrycks and Gimpel (2017), Geng et al. (2021), Vaze et al. (2022) | Post-hoc rejection via MSP, OpenMax Weibull tail fitting, or closed-set logit scaling | Unbounded softmax sectors compress confidence scores near 1.0 under near-OOD mimicry | Diagnoses MSP pile-up at $\tau^*=0.9980$ on FLS near-OOD twins | contrast / baseline |
| Sonar open-set recognition | Jiao et al. (2024), Li et al. (2026) | Imbalanced long-tail sonar OSR via logit/OpenMax calibration | Do not dissect single-center SVDD collapse vs. hyperspherical cosine triplet learning on MD-FLS | Complementary FLS OSR line; distinct benchmark and metric-learning focus (not directly compared on NKSID) | contrast |
| One-class boundaries | Scholkopf et al. (2001), Tax and Duin (2004), Chong et al. (2020) | Kernel hypersphere and OC-SVM anomaly boundaries without unknown training samples | Single global centers collapse when supervised features form separated high-norm radial lobes | Explains why Global Centroid gets `0.2371` AUROC while Nearest Centroid recovers to `0.9175` | baseline / direct |
| Deep metric embeddings | Hoffer and Ailon (2014), Schroff et al. (2015), Musgrave et al. (2020) | Compact angular clusters on $S^{d-1}$ with online hard triplet mining | Rarely evaluated jointly against MSP and SVDD collapse on near-OOD underwater FLS | Operationalizes $S^{127}$ triplet learning to achieve `0.9603` AUROC and `80.53%` `chain` detection | direct / software |
